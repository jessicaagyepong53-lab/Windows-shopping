Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2026

%COMPANY% respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We handle personal data in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number and a scrambled (hashed) version of your password.
  • What you post: ads, photos, messages, offers, reviews and reports.
  • Orders and delivery: items bought, delivery name, phone, address, GhanaPost digital address, delivery notes, and chosen day and time.
  • Payments: card and Mobile Money payments are handled by Paystack. We only receive the payment status and a reference, not your card number.
  • Seller payout details: your Mobile Money network, number and registered name, if you are a seller.
  • Technical data: a sign-in cookie that keeps you logged in, and your cart and wish list saved in your browser. We do not use advertising cookies. If you turn on phone alerts, we also store your device's notification address so we can send them. You can turn this off any time in your account.

2. Why we use it

To run your account, show your ads, process and deliver orders, take payments, pay sellers, send you codes and notices, keep the site safe, prevent fraud, answer your questions, and meet legal duties.

3. Who we share it with

  • Buyers and sellers: only what is needed to complete a sale, such as the delivery name, phone number and address, and the seller's public profile.
  • Service providers: Paystack (payments), our hosting provider, and email or SMS providers that deliver our messages.
  • Authorities: when the law requires it or to protect people from fraud or harm.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open and for as long as needed to handle orders, disputes, accounting and legal duties. After that we delete or anonymise it.

5. How we protect it

Passwords are stored hashed, connections use HTTPS, access to admin tools is limited, and we keep regular backups. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, private password.

7. Children

The site is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
